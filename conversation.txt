## Game Overview
A hilarious tower defense game where cute cats defend a Golden Tuna Can from household nightmares (cucumbers, vacuum cleaners, and the dreaded mailman)!

### Game Mechanics
- **Economy**: Start with 120 Kibble, earn from kills/combos/wave bonuses
- **3 Waves**: Cucumbers → Dust Bunnies + Vacuums → Mixed + Boss Mailman
- **Combo System**: Kill 2+ enemies in 2 seconds for bonus Kibble
- **Obstacles**: 3 destructible objects unlock premium tower positions
- **Tower Interaction**: Click placed cats to upgrade (3 levels) or sell (70% refund)
- **10 Sanity (lives)**: Game over when all leak through
